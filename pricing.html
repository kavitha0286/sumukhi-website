01 FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Can't find what you need? Talk to us — we usually reply the same day.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Minimums vary by product and branding method. Share your quantity in the enquiry form and we'll recommend options that fit — including smaller runs for leadership or VIP gifting.

How is our logo added to the products?

We offer laser engraving, screen printing and UV printing. The best method depends on the material — laser for metal and bamboo, screen or UV for colour logos on notebooks, bottles and bags. We'll share a digital mock-up for approval before production.

How long does an order take?

Lead time depends on quantity, stock and branding. We confirm a firm dispatch date with your quote — for festive seasons, we recommend enquiring a few weeks in advance.

Can we mix products to build a custom hamper?

Absolutely. Pick from any collection — notebooks, drinkware, tech, bags — and we'll assemble it in a gift box with custom inserts sized to your selection.

Do you deliver to multiple locations?

Yes. Send us a delivery list and we'll pack and ship to multiple offices or individual addresses.

Can we see samples before ordering?

Yes — samples can be arranged for shortlisted products so you can check finish and quality before confirming a bulk order.

Do you have eco-friendly options?

Our eco range includes bamboo and cork notebooks, seed-paper diaries, plantable seed pens, wheat-fibre mugs, glass bottles with bamboo lids and jute bags.
