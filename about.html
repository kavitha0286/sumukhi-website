01 Who we are

A gifting studio, not a catalogue.

Sumukhi helps organisations say thank you, welcome and congratulations — beautifully. We bring together notebooks, drinkware, tech, bags, plants and complete hampers, then brand, pack and deliver them so every gift feels considered.

Whether it's onboarding kits for new joiners, festive hampers for your whole team or mementos for a convocation, we work from your brief and budget to a shortlist you can say yes to.

  • Curated, not crowded

    A focused selection of gifts that look good, work well and last.

  • True to your brand

    Logos applied with the right method for every material, approved before production.

  • Presented beautifully

    Rigid gift boxes, custom inserts and thoughtful finishing touches.

  • One point of contact

    From shortlist to dispatch, one team handles your order end to end.

02 Branding

Your logo, applied the right way.

We match the branding method to the material, and share a digital mock-up for approval before anything is produced.

Laser engraving

Crisp, permanent marks on metal, bamboo, wood and leatherette — ideal for pens, keychains and card holders.

Screen printing

Bold, durable single- or multi-colour prints for bottles, bags and larger runs.

UV printing

Full-colour logos and artwork on notebooks, diaries, boxes and flat surfaces.

03 How it works

From brief to doorstep in four steps.

  1. 01

    Share your brief

    Tell us the occasion, quantity, budget and date. We'll shortlist products that fit.

  2. 02

    Curate & quote

    Receive a tailored selection with models, colours and a transparent per-unit quote.

  3. 03

    Brand & pack

    Your logo by laser engraving, screen or UV printing — packed in premium gift boxes.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Dispatched on schedule to one office or many locations, ready to hand out.
